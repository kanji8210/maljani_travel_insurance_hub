# 📖 Maljani Hub User Manual

## 1. Getting Started
Welcome to the Maljani Travel Insurance Hub. Whether you are a Travel Agent or a Solo Traveler, this guide will help you get covered in minutes.

## 2. Accessing the Portal
Visit the [Registration Page](/registration) to create an account.
- **Agents**: Select "Travel Agency" and provide your agency details. Your account will be reviewed by the Maljani team.
- **Travelers**: Select "Individual" to create a personal member account.

## 3. Purchasing Insurance
1.  **Select Dates**: Enter your departure and return dates in the Quote Wizard.
2.  **Select Region**: Choose your destination (e.g., Schengen, Africa).
3.  **Choose a Policy**: Compare available plans and prices based on your duration.
4.  **Complete Details**: Enter your passport and contact information exactly as they appear on your documents.
5.  **Payment**: Follow the secure payment instructions and enter your transaction reference.

## 4. Managing Your Policies
Log in to your **Dashboard** to:
- Download your active Policy Certificates.
- Generate QR-coded Embassy Letters for visa applications.
- Track commission status (Agents only).
- Renew or purchase new coverage.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions
- **Where is my certificate?** Once payment is verified by an admin, your certificate will appear in your dashboard and be sent via email.
- **Can I buy for my group?** Agents can purchase for multiple clients from their specific portal.
- **What if my dates change?** Contact support at [support@kipdevwp.tech](mailto:support@kipdevwp.tech).
